  chenchens190009/CSProm-KG

The local models/csprom_control.py documents the adaptations used to run that
reference under the experiment suite. Its metrics are local recipe controls,
not a claim to reproduce the original paper's tuned results.

helper.py credits the fastai label-smoothing implementation. Existing source
comments and attribution have been preserved.
